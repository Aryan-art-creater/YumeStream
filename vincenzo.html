"Vincenzo" is a 2021 South Korean television series that follows the story of Vincenzo Cassano, a Korean-born Italian mafia consigliere who returns to his motherland after the death of his adoptive father, the head of the Cassano Family. Adopted at the age of eight and raised in Italy, Vincenzo is a skilled lawyer and right-hand man to the mafia boss, but flees to Seoul after a power struggle within the family. His primary mission is to recover a hidden gold bullion worth ₩150 billion ($110 million) buried beneath Geumga Plaza, a commercial building illegally seized by the corrupt Babel Group, a pharmaceutical conglomerate. When he discovers that the building's demolition is imminent, he must use his mafia tactics and legal acumen to reclaim it and protect the gold. He forms an alliance with Hong Cha-young, a determined lawyer and the daughter of a murdered defense attorney who was investigating Babel's ties to a drug cartel. Together, they fight against the powerful corporation using unconventional methods, including espionage, intimidation, and recruiting the quirky tenants of Geumga Plaza as allies. The series blends crime drama.Together, they fight against the powerful corporation using unconventional methods, including espionage, intimidation, and recruiting the quirky tenants of Geumga Plaza as allies. The series blends crime drama.
