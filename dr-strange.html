Doctor Strange (2016) is a Marvel Studios superhero film directed by Scott Derrickson, serving as the 14th installment in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU). The film follows Dr. Stephen Strange, a brilliant but arrogant neurosurgeon portrayed by Benedict Cumberbatch, whose career is cut short after a car accident severely damages his hands, rendering him unable to perform surgery. Desperate to restore his hands, Strange turns from conventional medicine to a mysterious enclave known as Kamar-Taj in Nepal, where he encounters the Ancient One, played by Tilda Swinton, and begins training in the mystic arts. Through his training, Strange learns to manipulate reality using magical artifacts like the Eye of Agamotto, which contains the Time Stone, and the Cloak of Levitation, gaining abilities such as spellcasting, portal creation, and astral projection. He becomes involved in a conflict when Kaecilius, a former student of the Ancient One played by Mads Mikkelsen, steals sacred texts to summon Dormammu, a malevolent entity from the Dark Dimension, in an attempt to achieve eternal life. Kaecilius attacks the Sanctums in New York, London, and Hong Kong, which protect Earth from interdimensional threats. Kaecilius attacks the Sanctums in New York, London, and Hong Kong, which protect Earth from interdimensional threats. Kaecilius attacks the Sanctums in New York, London, and Hong Kong, which protect Earth from interdimensional threats.
