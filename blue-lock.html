Blue Lock is a Japanese manga series written by Muneyuki Kaneshiro and illustrated by Yusuke Nomura, serialized in Kodansha's Weekly Shōnen Magazine since August 2018. The story centers on a controversial training program initiated by the Japan Football Union after the national team's poor performance in the 2018 FIFA World Cup. The program, known as Blue Lock, is designed to create the world's greatest egotistic striker by isolating 300 of Japan's top young forwards in a prison-like facility. The facility, a massive pentagon-shaped building located on a cliffside in Japan, houses training fields, living quarters, and a central football arena capable of hosting professional matches. The protagonist, Yoichi Isagi, a high school forward who hesitated to take a decisive shot during a crucial game, is invited to Blue Lock after his team's elimination from the national qualifiers. There, he competes against 299 other elite strikers under the guidance of the enigmatic coach Jinpachi Ego, whose radical philosophy aims to destroy the "loser mentality" of Japanese football by fostering extreme individualism and ego.The facility, a massive pentagon-shaped building located on a cliffside in Japan, houses training fields, living quarters, and a central football arena capable of hosting professional matches.
