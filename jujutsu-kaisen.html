Jujutsu Kaisen is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Gege Akutami, serialized in Shueisha's shōnen manga magazine Weekly Shōnen Jump from March 2018 to September 2024, with its chapters collected in 30 tankōbon volumes. The story follows high school student Yuji Itadori, who becomes the host of Ryomen Sukuna, a powerful ancient curse, after swallowing one of Sukuna's preserved fingers to save his friends from a curse. To prevent Sukuna's full resurrection and protect humanity, Itadori joins the Tokyo Prefectural Jujutsu High School, a secret organization of Jujutsu Sorcerers tasked with eliminating curses. His mission is to locate and consume Sukuna's remaining fingers, which will ultimately lead to Sukuna's destruction and his own execution.In this world, all living beings emit a power source known as Cursed Energy, which arises from negative emotions and can accumulate to form malevolent spiritual entities called Curses, often drawing on traditional Japanese folklore. Jujutsu Sorcerers are individuals capable of controlling this energy, using it to perform Cursed Techniques and even create Domain Expansions—pocket dimensions that enhance their abilities and guarantee hits on their targets.
