Horimiya is a Japanese romantic comedy anime series that centers on two high school students, Kyouko Hori and Izumi Miyamura, who lead double lives and discover each other's hidden sides, leading to a blossoming friendship that evolves into romance. Kyouko is a popular, intelligent, and cheerful student who takes care of her younger brother at home, presenting a different, more down-to-earth persona away from school. In contrast, Izumi appears as a quiet, gloomy, and seemingly nerdy student at school, but outside of school, he is a laid-back, good-looking young man with numerous piercings and tattoos, and a punk-styled appearance. Their lives intersect when Izumi brings Kyouko's younger brother home after a nosebleed, leading to the accidental revelation of their true identities. They agree to keep their real selves secret from their peers, but as they spend more time together, they grow closer and eventually develop romantic feelings for each other.The series, produced by CloverWorks, aired from January to April 2021 and is based on the manga Hori-san to Miyamura-kun by Hiroki Adachi, which was adapted into the series under the title Horimiya by Daisuke Hagiwara.
