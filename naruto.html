Naruto is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Masashi Kishimoto, originally serialized in Shueisha's Weekly Shōnen Jump magazine from September 1999 to November 2014, with its 700 chapters collected into 72 tankōbon volumes. The story follows Uzumaki Naruto, a young, socially isolated ninja who dreams of becoming the Hokage, the leader of his Hidden Leaf Village (Konohagakure), in order to gain recognition from his peers. As a child, Naruto is ostracized by the villagers because the Nine-Tailed Demon Fox—a powerful malevolent creature that attacked Konoha—was sealed inside him by his father, the Fourth Hokage, Minato Namikaze, at the cost of his life. Despite this, Naruto's carefree, optimistic, and boisterous personality allows him to form deep bonds with fellow ninja, including his teammates Sasuke Uchiha and Sakura Haruno, and his mentor Kakashi Hatake. The narrative is divided into two main arcs: the first set in Naruto's pre-teen years (volumes 1–27), and the second during his teenage years (volumes 28–72), culminating in the Fourth Shinobi World War against the Akatsuki organization and the revelation of the true motives behind the Uchiha clan's destruction.
