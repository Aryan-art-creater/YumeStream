"Tsuki ga Kirei" (月がきれい; transl. As the Moon, So Beautiful) is a Japanese anime television series produced by Feel, which aired in Japan from April 6 to June 29, 2017. The series follows two junior high school students, Kotaro Azumi and Akane Mizuno, who become classmates for the first time in their third and final year of middle school. Kotaro, a literature club member aspiring to become a novelist and influenced by Osamu Dazai, is initially reserved but gradually opens up through his growing feelings for Akane, a track and field club member known for her easygoing nature and love of running. Their relationship develops slowly, particularly through shared responsibilities for a sports festival and communication via LINE. The story explores their emotional journey as they navigate first love, personal insecurities, and the complexities of adolescence. The series features a supporting cast of classmates, including Takumi Hira, who has been in love with Akane since first year, and Chinatsu Nishio, who develops feelings for Kotaro, creating a love rectangle that adds tension to the narrative.
