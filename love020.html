"Love O2O," also known as "A Smile is Beautiful" or "Wei Wei Yi Xiao Hen Qing Cheng," is a 2016 Chinese television drama series adapted from the web novel of the same name by Gu Man. The series is set in a university environment and centers on the romantic journey of Bei Wei Wei, a second-year computer science student known for her intelligence and beauty, and Xiao Nai, a fourth-year computer science student who is a top player in an online role-playing game called "A Chinese Ghost Story". Wei Wei, who plays under the username Luwei Weiwei in the game, is initially married to another player, Zhenshui Wuxiang, but after their virtual divorce, she faces online speculation about her identity, including rumors that she might be a man or unattractive due to her lack of a profile photo. Xiao Nai, the number one player in the Beijing server and the real-life senior at her university, discovers her identity and proposes to her in the game, becoming her new virtual husband. Their online partnership gradually evolves into a real-life romance after Xiao Nai reveals his identity to her, leading to a sweet and heartwarming campus love story.Xiao Nai, the number one player in the Beijing server and the real-life senior at her university, discovers her identity and proposes to her in the game, becoming her new virtual husband. Their online partnership gradually evolves into a real-life romance after Xiao Nai reveals his identity to her, leading to a sweet and heartwarming campus love story.
