"Money Heist" is a Spanish thriller television series created by Álex Pina, which follows a criminal mastermind known as the "Professor" who recruits a group of eight individuals, each adopting the alias of a city, to execute a meticulously planned heist. The primary objective is to rob the Royal Mint of Spain in Madrid, where the group takes 67 hostages and aims to print and escape with €2.4 billion over the course of eleven days. The robbers, dressed in red jumpsuits with masks depicting artist Salvador Dalí, operate from within the mint while the Professor coordinates the operation from outside. The series explores the psychological and emotional dynamics among the group, as well as their interactions with hostages and the police, particularly Inspector Raquel Murillo, who negotiates with the Professor. The heist is preceded by five months of intense preparation at an abandoned hunting estate in Toledo, during which the team is instructed to avoid personal relationships and maintain anonymity. The series explores the psychological and emotional dynamics among the group, as well as their interactions with hostages and the police, particularly Inspector Raquel Murillo, who negotiates with the Professor. The heist is preceded by five months of intense preparation at an abandoned hunting estate in Toledo, during which the team is instructed to avoid personal relationships and maintain anonymity.
