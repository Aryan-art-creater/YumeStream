Your Name is a 2016 Japanese animated romantic fantasy film written and directed by Makoto Shinkai, produced by CoMix Wave Films, and distributed by Toho. The film centers on two high school students, Taki Tachibana from Tokyo and Mitsuha Miyamizu from the rural town of Itomori in Gifu Prefecture, who begin to inexplicably swap bodies despite having never met. This phenomenon occurs intermittently, with each waking up in the other's body and living their life for the day before reverting upon sleep. The two communicate through notes left on phones, paper, and even on each other's skin, gradually forming a deep emotional connection. Mitsuha, who dreams of escaping her provincial life, uses Taki’s body to help her become more assertive and popular at school, while Taki, in Mitsuha’s body, helps her become more confident and even sets up a date for Taki with his coworker, Miki Okudera. The story takes a dramatic turn when the body-swapping ceases, and Taki realizes that Mitsuha’s town was destroyed three years earlier by fragments from the comet Tiamat.
