John Wick is a 2014 American action thriller film directed by Chad Stahelski and written by Derek Kolstad, starring Keanu Reeves as John Wick, a legendary hitman who comes out of retirement to seek revenge after a group of Russian gangsters steal his car and kill his puppy, a final gift from his recently deceased wife. The film follows John, a retired assassin who had left his violent career behind after marrying Helen, only to be thrust back into the criminal underworld when his dog is murdered and his prized 1969 Boss 429 Mustang is stolen. The incident triggers a brutal rampage as John, known in the underworld as "Baba Yaga," uses his unparalleled combat skills to exact vengeance against those responsible, including Iosef Tarasov, the son of crime boss Viggo Tarasov, and his associates. The story is set within a hidden world of assassins governed by strict rules and rituals, with the Continental hotel serving as a neutral ground where criminal business is forbidden. The incident triggers a brutal rampage as John, known in the underworld as "Baba Yaga," uses his unparalleled combat skills to exact vengeance against those responsible, including Iosef Tarasov, the son of crime boss Viggo Tarasov, and his associates. The story is set within a hidden world of assassins governed by strict rules and rituals, with the Continental hotel serving as a neutral ground where criminal business is forbidden.
