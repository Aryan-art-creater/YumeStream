Weak Hero (Korean: 약한영웅) is a South Korean television series based on the Naver webtoon of the same name by Seopass and Kim Jin-seok (Razen), which was published in 2018. The series, written and directed by Yoo Soo-min with Kim Jin-seok and Park Dan-hee, stars Park Ji-hoon as Yeon Si-eun, a top-performing student who ranks in the top 1% of his class and is intensely focused on studying. Despite being physically weak, Si-eun refuses to back down from bullies, particularly Jeon Yeong-bin, who targets him after losing a math competition. Using his quick decision-making skills, knowledge of physics, and awareness of his surroundings, Si-eun defends himself with intelligence and psychological tactics, often using everyday objects to his advantage. When Si-eun faces escalating violence and grave danger, he receives unexpected help from Ahn Su-ho, the strongest fighter in his class, and Oh Beom-seok, the tormented son of a wealthy assemblyman. The three form a close friendship and band together to survive the violent environment of their high school, learning what it truly means to be strong. The series is categorized as a coming-of-age action drama thriller, featuring intense bullying scenes and violent altercations, while emphasizing themes of friendship, loyalty, and personal growth.
