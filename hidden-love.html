Hidden Love (Chinese: 偷偷藏不住) is a 2023 Chinese television drama starring Zhao Lusi and Chen Zheyuan. It is based on the Chinese web novel Secretly, Secretly; But Unable to Hide It by Zhu Yi. The series aired on Youku from June 20 until July 6, 2023 and on Netflix, starting from June 29, 2023.Hidden Love has been praised for the strong on‑screen chemistry between its leads, Zhao Lusi and Chen Zheyuan, which many critics and viewers say gives the central romance authenticity and emotional depth. The series revolves around the life of Sang Zhi (Zhao Lusi), a young girl who develops a crush on Duan Jiaxu (Chen Zheyuan), who happens to be her older brother Sang Yan's (Victor Ma) bestfriend. Despite the initial infatuation, their relationship takes a natural progression as time passes.As the story unfolds, Sang Zhi's feelings for Duan Jiaxu deepen, transcending her initial crush and blossoming into genuine love., which many critics and viewers say gives the central romance authenticity and emotional depth.
