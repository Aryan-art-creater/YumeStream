Race 3 is a 2018 Indian Hindi-language action thriller film directed by Remo D'Souza and produced by Ramesh Taurani and Salman Khan under Tips Industries and Salman Khan Films. The film serves as the standalone sequel and third installment of the Race film series, which began with Race (2008) and Race 2 (2013). It stars an ensemble cast including Salman Khan, Anil Kapoor, Bobby Deol, Jacqueline Fernandez, Daisy Shah, and Saqib Saleem. The story centers on a dysfunctional family involved in crime, particularly focusing on Shamsher Singh (Anil Kapoor), a powerful Indian arms dealer based in Abu Dhabi who owns a private island, Al-Shifa, for trading arms and narcotics. His stepson, Sikander "Sikku" Singh (Salman Khan), is entrusted with a high-stakes heist to recover a hard disk containing incriminating videos of Indian politicians engaged in adulterous acts, stored in a Cambodian bank vault. This mission becomes a catalyst for unraveling deep-seated family secrets and betrayals.Race 3 is a 2018 Indian Hindi-language action thriller film directed by Remo D'Souza and produced by Ramesh Taurani and Salman Khan.
