ReLIFE is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Yayoiso, originally released in webtoon format on the Comico website from October 12, 2013, to March 16, 2018, and compiled into 15 tankōbon volumes by Earth Star Entertainment. The story follows Arata Kaizaki, a 27-year-old man who has become unemployed after quitting his job at a "black company" following only three months of employment, and is struggling to find new work due to his brief tenure. After being financially cut off by his parents, Arata is approached by Ryō Yoake, who offers him a chance to participate in the ReLIFE Experiment. This experiment involves taking a special pill that makes Arata appear to be a 17-year-old high school student, allowing him to experience a second chance at youth and life by enrolling in high school for one year. The goal is to help him rediscover his identity and overcome personal failures, particularly stemming from a traumatic incident at his previous job where he tried to help a female superior, Michiru, who was harassed by male coworkers, only to see her eventually commit suicide.
