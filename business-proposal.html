"Business Proposal" (Korean: 사내맞선) is a South Korean romantic comedy television series based on the web novel of the same title written by HaeHwa. The series, created by StudioS and directed by Park Seon-ho, stars Ahn Hyo-seop as Kang Tae-moo, the CEO of Go Food, and Kim Se-jeong as Shin Ha-ri, an employee at the company. The story follows Shin Ha-ri, who agrees to replace her friend Jin Young-seo on a blind date to avoid being pressured by her friend's father, but is shocked to discover that her date is actually her boss, Kang Tae-moo. Tae-moo, under pressure from his grandfather Kang Da-goo to find a suitable marriage partner, decides to marry the woman he meets on the blind date to avoid further dates, without knowing she is his employee. Shin Ha-ri, desperate for money to help her financially struggling parents, accepts Tae-moo's proposal to pretend to be his fiancée in exchange for 800,000 won per date. To maintain her cover, she adopts the fake identity of Shin Geum-hui, a character from a popular fictional drama, while navigating the challenges of their fake relationship. Shin Ha-ri, desperate for money to help her financially struggling parents, accepts Tae-moo's proposal to pretend to be his fiancée in exchange for 800,000 won per date. To maintain her cover, she adopts the fake identity of Shin Geum-hui, a character from a popular fictional drama, while navigating the challenges of their fake relationship.
