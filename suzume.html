Suzume (Japanese: すずめの戸締まり, Hepburn: Suzume no Tojimari; lit. 'Suzume's Locking Up') is a 2022 Japanese animated coming-of-age fantasy adventure film written and directed by Makoto Shinkai, serving as the third and final installment of his disaster trilogy following Your Name (2016) and Weathering with You (2019). The film centers on 17-year-old high school girl Suzume Iwato, who lives with her aunt Tamaki in a town in Kyushu, Japan. After meeting a mysterious young man named Souta Munakata, who is searching for abandoned locations with doors, Suzume discovers a free-standing door in a ruined onsen resort. When she opens it, she sees a starlit field she cannot enter, and the door's opening triggers a series of disasters across Japan as a colossal, supernatural worm begins to emerge from the portal. Souta, a "Closer" tasked with sealing these doors to prevent earthquakes, is injured and transformed into a three-legged chair by a talking cat named Daijin, who is revealed to be a "keystone" that seals the worm.
