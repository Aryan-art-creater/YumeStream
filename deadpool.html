Deadpool is a 2016 American superhero film directed by Tim Miller in his directorial debut and written by Rhett Reese and Paul Wernick, serving as a spin-off of the X-Men film series and its eighth installment. The film stars Ryan Reynolds as Wade Wilson, a former special forces operative turned mercenary who is diagnosed with terminal cancer and undergoes a rogue experiment that grants him accelerated healing abilities but leaves him severely disfigured. After being subjected to brutal torture and left for dead, Wilson survives and adopts the alias Deadpool, embarking on a quest for revenge against the man responsible for his condition, Francis Freeman, also known as Ajax. The film blends action, comedy, and science fiction, showcasing Deadpool's dark, twisted sense of humor and his relentless pursuit of justice. It features a supporting cast including Morena Baccarin as his fiancée Vanessa, Ed Skrein as Ajax, T.J.
